The Problem

Why VanMitra-AI?

Tribal communities face systemic barriers in securing forest land rights

01

Complex Documentation

FRA Rule 13 requirements are ambiguous. Claims are frequently rejected with poorly-explained reasons, leaving communities helpless and without legal recourse.

02

Meeting Manipulation

Gram Sabha meeting quorums and consent resolutions are often manipulated or backdated to enable illegal land diversion, with no digital audit trail.

03

No Forest Monitoring

Communities lack tools to monitor their forest boundaries for encroachment once rights are granted. Illegal deforestation goes undetected for months.

04

Online-Only Portals

Existing government portals are online-only, unavailable in Indic languages, and offer no tamper-proofing — completely unusable in low-connectivity rural areas.

Core Modules

Three Powerful Modules

A complete governance ecosystem for tribal communities

⚖️
Module A

AI Legal Assistant

OCR + RAG-based pipeline that scores evidence, drafts FRA claim forms, and generates appeal drafts for rejected claims — all in local languages.

  • Evidence Completeness Score
  • Auto-drafted FRA claim forms
  • Appeal generation with deadline countdown
  • Document verification via OCR
🛰️
Module B

Satellite Digital Fencing

Offline maps of forest boundaries with satellite-based encroachment and deforestation alerts — powered by Sentinel-2 imagery and OpenStreetMap.

  • Offline boundary maps
  • Sentinel-2 satellite imagery
  • Encroachment / deforestation alerts
  • GPS-based geofencing
🔐
Module C

Gram Sabha Integrity Ledger

Digitises village meetings with GPS geofencing, on-device face recognition, automated quorum checks, and a SHA-256 tamper-proof ledger.

  • GPS-tagged meeting creation
  • On-device face recognition attendance
  • SHA-256 hash-chained ledger
  • Trilingual PDF export of minutes
🌐

Workflow

How It Works

A seamless, step-by-step governance experience

1

User Login

User logs in using valid credentials. Choose language → Register with name/phone/role (Villager or Secretary/Admin).

2

Data Collection

Claim details or Gram Sabha data are collected. Select claim type, enter land details, upload evidence — the app scores completeness live.

3

AI Verification

AI verifies documents, generates legal drafts, and validates evidence. OCR scans rejection orders and drafts appeal with filing deadline countdown.

4

Satellite Monitoring

Satellite module monitors forest boundaries. Encroachment and deforestation alerts trigger automatically using Sentinel-2 imagery.

5

Secure Ledger

Meeting records are securely stored using SHA-256 tamper-evident ledger. Anyone can verify the full chain to confirm no record was altered.

6

Auto Sync

Data synchronises automatically when internet connectivity becomes available — using Firebase cloud sync with automatic retry queue.

Viability

Feasibility & Viability

Designed for real-world deployment constraints

🔧 Technical

  • Built on proven, widely-adopted tech: Flutter, Firebase, FastAPI, TensorFlow Lite
  • Offline-first design (Hive local DB) works in low-connectivity tribal/forest regions
  • Falls back to offline templates when AI/connectivity unavailable — no hard dependency
  • Trilingual support (English, Hindi, Marathi, partial Konkani) fits real user base

⚙️ Operational

  • Assumes only a mid-range Android device with GPS/camera — low hardware barrier
  • Single-village pilot (Ozhar, Jawhar Taluka) already scoped as proof of concept
  • Four-language localization fits the real user base without training overhead
  • Villagers and Secretaries have separate roles with tailored UX flows

💰 Economic

  • Uses free/open-source tools (Flutter, FastAPI, Hive, Tesseract, FAISS) — low licensing cost
  • Firebase free-tier-friendly backend; Docker-based deployment keeps infra cost low
  • No specialized hardware needed beyond a standard Android smartphone
  • Scalable without significant incremental cost per village added

⚠️ Risks & Roadmap

  • OCR accuracy drops on poor-quality documents → manual review fallback available
  • AI drafting/voice transcription needs connectivity → offline templates as backup
  • Face recognition affected by poor lighting → manual override available
  • Roadmap: satellite pipeline, GPS boundary mapping, NGO portal, SMS sync, more languages
