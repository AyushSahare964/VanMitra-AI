About Tech for Tomorrow
=======================

Discover the mission and vision behind the IEEE Tech for Tomorrow hackathon.

Tech for Tomorrow
-----------------

Tech for Tomorrow is more than a competition—it is a platform that inspires young innovators to step beyond classrooms and address real-world challenges. The initiative encourages students to observe societal issues firsthand and develop affordable, high-impact technological solutions that can bring meaningful change to lives and communities.

### Bridging the Gap

In India, many rural and urban communities still face limited access to clean water, healthcare, and renewable energy. While technology grows rapidly, these benefits often bypass marginalized populations. Tech for Tomorrow empowers students to apply their technical skills to these real-life problems, fostering innovation, inclusivity, and long-term social impact.

Our Mission
-----------

To empower multidisciplinary student teams to design and deploy ready-to-use software and hardware prototypes by engaging directly with communities, solving real-world problems through innovation, collaboration, and resource-efficient technology, and supporting the most impactful solutions with funding for large-scale deployment.

### Why We Organize This Hackathon

In a rapidly evolving technological landscape, we need innovators who can think beyond conventional boundaries. This hackathon brings together students, professionals, and mentors to tackle real-world challenges and create meaningful solutions.

**The Process**

What We Are Looking For
-----------------------

Each multidisciplinary team, guided by a faculty mentor and supported by a community representative, will conduct field visits, identify problems, and build working prototypes from scratch using limited resources. The **top 10 shortlisted teams** will showcase their innovations. This final exhibition offers an engaging platform for live demonstration, jury evaluation, and networking with industry experts.

**Final Deployment**

The top three teams will receive funding support for full-scale deployment of their solutions in the communities they serve.
